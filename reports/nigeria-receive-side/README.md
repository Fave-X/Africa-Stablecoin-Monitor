# Nigeria, receive side: what small stablecoin transfers look like after 5 Nov

*Draft in progress.*

Focus: transfers under $10k, the sizes families and freelancers actually receive.

## Sections

1. The rail picture for recipients: small-band volume on Tron USDT vs Ethereum, before and after 5 Nov; what a $200 transfer costs on each rail.
2. The corridor in numbers (cited desk research: World Bank / CBN remittance data, main UK and US sending operators).
3. The recipient journey today: payout methods, FX spread vs the parallel rate, card acceptance, reasons to hold a balance.
4. What the CBN framework means for wallet providers serving Nigerian recipients (questions, not legal advice; links to the circular).
5. Limits: chain data can't tell a remittance from any other small transfer.
